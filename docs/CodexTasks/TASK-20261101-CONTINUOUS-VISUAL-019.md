## Producer's standing direction
For each future gameplay, controls, mobility, QA or bug-fix request, **consider and request one small, independently verifiable visual improvement** toward the previously approved SD mech concept-art direction. Repeated passes are expected; one or two iterations are not enough. Do not assert exact concept match while original art is unavailable.

## Rules
- Focus on silhouette and form first: head-to-body ratio, broad shoulder/chest/pelvis masses, sturdy leg/foot volumes, believable joint articulation and animation, readable SD proportions; then materials, lighting, effects and camera.
- **Never substitute decorative rods, random attachments or extra greebles for meaningful form improvement.** Do not duplicate already finished work or regress Kestrel/ground fixes.
- For each implementation task, propose a **small visual subtask** only if safely compatible with its files, source baseline and test scope. If conflicting or risky, queue a separate nonoverlapping visual candidate instead of blocking urgent functional work.
- Each visual edit must be reversible and traceable: record before/after captures from identical camera, pose, lighting, and scale, exact branch/full SHA, Unity build and built-player smoke. Preserve previous images and do not claim improvement solely from higher polygon/voxel counts.
- Keep main untouched; use latest verified candidate pinned to exact SHA. Do not invent approvals, final silhouette sign-off, local build availability or original concept art.
- Prepare a short prioritized visual backlog and checklist reusable by hourly PM. No source edits in this check; report to codex/task-reports.
