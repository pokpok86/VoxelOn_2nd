## Purpose
Make the newly separated knee/ankle pivots visibly move in gameplay, even if animation is rough. Keep the complete robot silhouette in view during comparisons, not only isolated joint movement.

## Why
The existing `leftLeg/rightLeg` swing moved the entire leg rigidly. Articulated knee and ankle rotation makes the new joint hierarchy useful without modifying collision or combat. The method resolves named child pivots with Transform.Find for now; optimize/cache after correctness is validated.
